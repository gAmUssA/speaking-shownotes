## Abstract

Imagine a world where analytics is not just for the boardroom but for everyone, everywhere, every moment. Pinot is not your average OLAP database; it’s a turbocharged engine designed to power features that users interact with, delivering insights faster than you can say “real-time.” We’re talking about lightning-fast queries, sky-high concurrency, and data fresher than your morning coffee.
In this talk, we’ll integrate Apache Pulsar, Flink, and Pinot for real-time analytics. You’ll get the inside scoop on how Pinot is built, how to feed it with data from Pulsar or Kafka, and how to use Flink for pre-processing. We’ll showcase how Pinot changes the game, making analytics available at everyone’s fingertips. Get ready to explore the exciting world of real-time analytics with Apache Pulsar, Flink, and Pinot!
