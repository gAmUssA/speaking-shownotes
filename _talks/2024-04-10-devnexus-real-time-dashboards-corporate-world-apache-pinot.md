## Abstract

This isn’t your typical tech talk—it’s a story of the journey into how big players like LinkedIn, Uber Eats, and Stripe are mastering the art of real-time data.
Viktor is here to demystify Apache Pinot’s superpowers, showing you how it instantly transforms mountains of data into actionable insights. Business decision-makers have long had access to dashboards and reports, but now analytics can be made available to users as features of stickier, more engaging applications. With stories, insights, and a touch of humor, Viktor will unpack the cool features of Apache Pinot, including the Star-Tree Index, and show you why it’s a game-changer in data strategy.
This session is for everyone, whether you’re a data geek, a business guru, or just curious about the future of tech. Viktor’s dynamic style will keep you on the edge of your seat, eager to put these insights into action. So, buckle up and get ready to be wowed by the power of real-time analytics with Apache Pinot – it will be a blast!
